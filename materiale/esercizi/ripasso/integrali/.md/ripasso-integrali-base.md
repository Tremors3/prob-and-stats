## $$ \textcolor{red}{\text{Ripasso integrali}} $$

#### Integrali Immediati

1. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= 3x^2+4x-1
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
\int f(x)\,dx
&= \left(x^3 + 2x^2 - x\right) + c
\end{aligned}
}$$

2. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= x^5 - \frac2x
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
\int f(x)\,dx
&= \left(\frac{x^6}6 - 2\ln{|x|}\right) + c
\end{aligned}
}$$

3. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= \sqrt{x} + \frac1{\sqrt{x}}
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
\int f(x)\,dx
&= \int \left(x^{\frac12} + x^{-\frac12}\right)\,dx \\
&= \int x^{\frac12}\,dx + \int x^{-\frac12}\,dx \\
&= \left(\frac{x^{\frac32}}{\frac32} + \frac{x^{\frac12}}{\frac12}\right) + c \\
&= \left(\frac23x^{\frac32} + 2x^{\frac12}\right) + c \\
&= \left(\frac23\sqrt[2]{x^3} + 2\sqrt x\right) + c \\
\end{aligned}
}$$

4. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= e^x
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
\int f(x)\,dx
&= e^x + c
\end{aligned}
}$$

5. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= e^{-x}
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
\int f(x)\,dx
&= \int e^{-x}\,dx \\
&= -\int -e^{-x}\,dx \\
&= -e^{-x} + c
\end{aligned}
}$$
