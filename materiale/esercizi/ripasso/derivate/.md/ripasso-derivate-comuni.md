## $$ \textcolor{red}{\text{Ripasso derivate comuni}\\\text{in statistica}} $$

#### Esercizi

1. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= x^2e^{-x} + 3\ln(x^2-1)- \frac{\ln(x)}{x}
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
f'(x)
&= 2xe^{-x} + x^2(-e^{-x}) + 3\frac1{x^2-1}2x - \frac{\frac1xx-\ln(x)}{x^2} \\
&= e^{-x}(2x-x^2) + \frac{6x}{x^2-1} - \frac{1-\ln(x)}{x^2}
\end{aligned}
}$$

2. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= (x^2+1)^3e^{-x^2} + x\ln(e^x+1)
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
f'(x)
&= 3(x^2+1)^22xe^{-x^2} + (x^2+1)^3(-2xe^{-x^2}) + \ln(e^x+1) + x\frac1{e^x+1}e^x \\
&= 6xe^{-x^2}(x^2+1)^2 - 2xe^{-x^2}(x^2+1)^3 + \ln(e^x+1) + xe^x\frac1{e^x+1} \\
&= 2xe^{-x^2}(x^2+1)^2(2-x^2) + \ln(e^x+1) + xe^x\frac1{e^x+1}
\end{aligned}
}$$

3. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= \frac{x^2e^{-x}}{\ln(x)} + \ln(x^3+1)-\frac1{x^2+1}
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
f'(x)
&= \frac{(2xe^{-x} - x^2e^{-x})\ln(x) - x^2e^{-x}\frac1x }{(\ln(x))^2} + \frac1{x^3+1}3x^2 + (x^2+1)^{-2}2x
\end{aligned}
}$$

4. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= x^3e^{-\frac{x^2}2} + \frac{\ln(x^2+1)}x - x\ln(x)
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
f'(x)
&= 3x^2e^{-\frac{x^2}2} + x^3(-x)e^{-\frac{x^2}2} \frac{\frac{2x}{x^2+1}x + \ln(x^2+1)}{x^2} - (\ln(x) + 1)
\end{aligned}
}$$

5. $$\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
f(x)
&= (x^2+1)^2e^{-x} +\frac{x\ln(x)}{x^2+1} + \ln\left(e^{-x^2}+1\right)
\\[4pt]\textit{Sol.}\quad
f'(x)
&= 2(x^2+1)2xe^{-x} + (x^2+1)^2(-e^{-x}) + \frac{(\ln(x) + \frac1xx)(x^2+1) - x\ln(x)2x}{(x^2+1)^2} + \frac1{e^{-x^2}+1}(-2xe^{-x^2})
\end{aligned}
}$$
