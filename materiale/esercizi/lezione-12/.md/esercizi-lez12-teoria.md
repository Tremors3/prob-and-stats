#### Esercizio 3

##### Risoluzione

Sia $X\sim Par(\alpha, 1), calcolare $\text{Var}(X)$$

$$
E[X] = \int_0^{+\infty} x\alpha x^{-\alpha-1} dx
= \int_0^{+\infty} \alpha x^{-\alpha}
= \alpha \left[ \frac{x^{-\alpha+1}}{-\alpha+1} \right]_0^{+\infty}
$$
